GRAVEYARD ORBIT MAPS — BATTLE MOON + COW ABDUCTION (0.2.0)
=========================================================

WHAT THIS IS
  Two original custom contracts for Graveyard Orbit, built from hex cells:

  BATTLE MOON (59,819 cells)
    A planet-killer battlestation seen in 3/4 view from below-right. Black
    panel grout and open bays (31.9% of the map) surround a wide equatorial
    trench. A superlaser dish sits north of the equator and left of centre.

  COW ABDUCTION (50,221 cells)
    A saucer/dome UFO tractor-beaming a cow, with four gold-capped entrances
    and a 3-wide black maze.

  This package includes BepInEx, a small loader plugin and both maps; no
  compiler or separate mod loader is needed.

REQUIREMENTS
  - Windows 10/11, or Linux / Steam Deck (Proton)
  - Graveyard Orbit installed through Steam (AppID 4654120)

INSTALL (SAME ON WINDOWS AND LINUX)
  1. Close Graveyard Orbit.
  2. In Steam, right-click Graveyard Orbit > Manage > Browse local files.
     That opens the game folder (the one containing GraveyardOrbit.exe).
  3. Download the release zip:
       https://github.com/Grokent/graveyard_orbit_maps/releases/latest/download/GraveyardOrbit-Maps-0.2.0.zip
  4. Extract EVERYTHING from this zip into the game folder that opened in
     step 2. It only adds files; no original game file is replaced or
     modified:
       winhttp.dll
       doorstop_config.ini
       .doorstop_version
       changelog.txt
       BepInEx/...
  5. Linux / Steam Deck (Proton) ONLY: add this launch option once
     (game > Properties > Launch Options):
       WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%
     Windows needs no launch options.
  6. Launch the game through Steam.

PLAY
  New Game > BATTLE MOON or COW ABDUCTION (both appear at the top of the
  contract list). Start a NEW contract after updating; continuing an old save
  keeps its destroyed-cell history.

  BATTLE MOON
    The ship approaches from the planet side (bottom of the map). The black
    trench runs across the equator; the dish is above it, left of centre.
    Follow the black grout between hull panels or breach straight through.
    - 31.9% black: panel grout, open panel bays, and a 2,889-cell trench.
    - 32 hidden rooms, 29 artifacts (power-up rolls), 140 glyphs (beacons for
      2 players x 20 relics), 96 salvage crates, 399 material veins.
    - $1000 starting money.

  COW ABDUCTION
    The ship starts on the planet side of the cow.
    - Four gold-capped entrances at the leg bottoms lead into a 3-wide black
      maze. The south is gold/blue and easy; the north is purple/pink and hard.
    - 24 hidden rooms, 20 artifacts, 120 glyphs, 64 salvage crates, 298
      material veins.

UNINSTALL
  Delete BepInEx/plugins/GraveyardOrbit.UfoCow/
  Remove winhttp.dll, doorstop_config.ini, .doorstop_version, changelog.txt and
  BepInEx/ only if you do not use any other BepInEx mod.
